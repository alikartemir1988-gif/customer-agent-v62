# Customer Agent V6 — Commercial Offer

## Current price policy

- New prospects: USD 6,500 asking price for an agreed custom deployment.
- Negotiation may reach USD 5,000 under the approved price policy and written scope.
- Honor USD 5,000 already quoted to existing prospects; do not raise their quote.
- The price is an offer, not an independently validated valuation or evidence of ROI.

## Suitable buyer

An Arabic- or English-speaking merchant with repetitive catalog/delivery questions and orders
arriving through Telegram, who needs a customized, deployed order workflow and
operator tools. The current core is not a complete global enterprise support suite.

## Proposed standard delivery, to be agreed in writing

- Deployment and verification of the Customer Agent core.
- Merchant branding, Arabic/English wording, product catalog and delivery-area setup.
- Complete Arabic and English order conversations with product, optional color,
  quantity, contact details, review, explicit confirmation, edits and cancellation.
- Telegram bot integration and explicit order review/confirmation.
- Protected order API, status history, and operator dashboard.
- Initial merchant-reviewed FAQ setup, including source labels/links.
- Staff follow-up queue with captured order context; operators reply through the
  customer's channel. It is not a live-agent chat console.
- Basic counts of reviewed FAQ replies, no-answer fallbacks, and staff requests.
- Basic operator instructions and 14 days of post-launch defect support.

Agree on catalog size, FAQ count, acceptance examples, support hours, delivery date,
software/IP rights and any source-code handover before accepting payment.
Do not promise exclusive ownership without an explicit agreement.

## Running costs and additional scope

The asking price does not make ongoing operation free or unlimited. Hosting,
database service, model use, quotas and third-party channel charges depend on the
buyer's selected setup. Our current setup uses free plans and local replies when
Gemini is unavailable; free-tier limits and availability still apply.

Messenger webhook support exists in code; a buyer's page/account connection must
be configured and verified before selling it as an active integration. WhatsApp,
voice, CRM/ERP connections, website crawling/PDF ingestion, semantic knowledge
search, SSO/team roles, and ongoing managed
support require separately agreed work. Do not describe them as delivered features.

## Claims we can demonstrate

- Arabic and English catalog, delivery and complete order conversation examples.
- English public demo interface at `/?lang=en`; both languages use the same core.
- Only approved FAQ entries are used for exact question/alias matches, with a
  source title and optional link. Source documents are merchant supplied.
- A customer's staff request creates a persistent private follow-up ticket.
- Public demo requests are simulated and do not create real orders/tickets.

Do not claim automatic learning, a guaranteed resolution rate, elimination of
hallucinations across all model replies, proven savings, or enterprise availability.

## Why a buyer might accept USD 6,500

The price must be justified by buyer-specific configuration, deployment, acceptance
testing and agreed delivery/support obligations. A generic chatbot demonstration
does not establish that value. Use the buyer's actual volume, costs and required
workflow to assess value. Illustrative savings are assumptions, not promises.

The current competitors' official pages include lower-entry subscription options;
see [the dated comparison](docs/MARKET_COMPARISON_2026-10-05.md). Subscription fees,
custom delivery scope, operating costs and ownership terms are different dimensions.
