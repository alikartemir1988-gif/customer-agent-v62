# Customer Agent — Commercial Offer

## Offer

A ready-to-deploy Arabic conversational sales agent for Telegram that answers product questions, captures orders and provides a protected order-management API.

## Target customers

- E-commerce merchants
- Social-commerce sellers
- Local distributors
- Electronics and appliance stores
- Cosmetics and fashion sellers
- Businesses receiving repetitive Telegram inquiries

## Recommended pricing

### Business Deployment — USD 5,000 minimum

Includes:

- Customer Agent software deployment
- Merchant branding and Arabic copy customization
- Initial product catalog configuration
- Delivery-area configuration
- Telegram bot integration
- Order capture workflow
- Protected order-management API
- Deployment verification
- Basic operator documentation
- 14 days of post-launch defect support

Not included unless separately agreed:

- Hosting fees
- Telegram/third-party fees
- Custom ERP/CRM integrations
- WhatsApp Business Platform fees/integration
- Major custom feature development
- Ongoing managed support

## Suggested higher-value packages

- Business: $5,000 — standard deployment
- Pro: $7,500 — deployment plus merchant-specific workflow customization and reporting
- Enterprise: $12,000+ — integrations, database migration, monitoring and custom workflows

## Sales pitch

**Arabic:**

وكيل مبيعات آلي يعمل على تيليغرام، يرد على أسئلة العملاء عن المنتجات والأسعار والتوصيل، يجمع بيانات الطلب بشكل منظم، ويسجل الطلبات تلقائياً مع لوحة API محمية لإدارة الطلبات. يتم تخصيصه باسم المتجر ومنتجاته ومناطق التوصيل الخاصة به.

**English:**

An Arabic-first Telegram sales automation system that answers repetitive product, pricing and delivery questions, guides customers through checkout-style conversations, captures structured orders, and exposes a protected order-management API. It is customized and deployed for each merchant.

## Qualification questions

Before quoting a buyer, confirm:

1. How many products/SKUs do they sell?
2. How many customer conversations do they receive per day?
3. Which countries/cities do they deliver to?
4. Do they need Telegram only or additional channels?
5. Do they need CRM/ERP integration?
6. Who will manage confirmed orders?
7. What hosting/compliance requirements do they have?

## Commercial rule

Do not quote or accept a software sale below USD 5,000 without the owner's explicit approval. Scope, delivery date, support obligations, payment method and software/IP rights must be written down before accepting payment.
